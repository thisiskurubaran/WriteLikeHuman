# Before and after

Everyday examples, one or two per area. All names, companies, and numbers are made up. Each module also has its own example inside it.

## Rewrite: a work message

Before:

> I hope this message finds you well! I wanted to reach out regarding the quarterly report. It's important to note that the deadline has been moved to 15 March. Please ensure that all inputs are submitted accordingly. Let me know if you have any questions!

After:

> Hi team, the quarterly report deadline has moved to 15 March. Please send me your inputs by then.

What I changed
- Removed the stock opening and closing lines.
- Cut "It's important to note that", which added nothing.
- Kept the date exactly as it was.

## Check: a short paragraph

Text:

> Embarking on this journey has been a truly transformative experience, allowing me to grow both personally and professionally.

Check:

```
Overall: This sentence sounds generated. It is all general claims
and no detail.

What stands out
- "Embarking on this journey" is a stock phrase
- "truly transformative" makes a big claim with nothing behind it
- "both personally and professionally" is a common filler pair

Want me to fix it? Tell me one thing that changed for you and
I'll build the sentence around that.
```

## Email: a polite no

Before:

> Thank you so much for thinking of me for this exciting opportunity! After careful consideration, I regret to inform you that I will not be able to take on this project at this time due to my current commitments. I truly appreciate your understanding and hope we can collaborate in the future!

After:

> Thanks for thinking of me. I can't take this on right now, I'm fully booked until June. I'd be glad to hear about the next one.

## Formal letter: leave application (Indian format)

Before (body only):

> I am writing to formally request a leave of absence. I hope this request finds you in good health. Due to unforeseen family circumstances, I will be unable to attend the office.

After (body only):

> I request leave from 12 to 14 August as I need to travel to Madurai for a family function. I have handed over my pending work to Ravi Kumar. Kindly grant me leave for these three days.

(The original gave no dates or reason. In real use, ask the user for them instead of filling them in. Here they are shown as if the user provided them.)

## Reading level: simpler for a parent

Before:

> Your policy renewal is due. Failure to remit the premium by the due date may result in a lapse of coverage.

After:

> Your insurance needs to be renewed. If you don't pay by the due date, the insurance will stop.

## Hinglish

Before (too formal for a family group):

> Kripya dhyaan dein, kal subah 10 baje sabhi parivaar ke sadasya ghar par upasthit rahein.

After:

> Kal subah 10 baje sab ghar pe rehna, okay?

(Kept the time and the Roman script. Changed only the stiff, textbook Hindi.)

## Spreadsheet
