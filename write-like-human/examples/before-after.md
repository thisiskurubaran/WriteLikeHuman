# Before and after

Everyday examples, one or two per area. All names, companies, and numbers are made up. Each module also has its own example inside it.

## Rewrite: a work message

Before:

> I hope this message finds you well! I wanted to reach out regarding the quarterly report. It's important to note that the deadline has been moved to 15 March. Please ensure that all inputs are submitted accordingly. Let me know if you have any questions!

After:

> Hi team, the quarterly report deadline has moved to 15 March. Please send me your inputs by then.

What I changed
- Removed the stock opening and closing lines.
- Cut "It's important to note that", which added nothing.
- Kept the date exactly as it was.

## Check: a short paragraph

Text:

> Embarking on this journey has been a truly transformative experience, allowing me to grow both personally and professionally.

Check:

```
Overall: This sentence sounds generated. It is all general claims
and no detail.

What stands out
- "Embarking on this journey" is a stock phrase
- "truly transformative" makes a big claim with nothing behind it
- "both personally and professionally" is a common filler pair

Want me to fix it? Tell me one thing that changed for you and
I'll build the sentence around that.
```

## Essay: helping with the student's own draft

Student's line:

> Since the dawn of time, technology has played a pivotal role in shaping the multifaceted landscape of human existence.

Suggestion (side by side):

```
1. "Since the dawn of time, technology has played a pivotal role
   in shaping the multifaceted landscape of human existence."
   -> Start with your own example instead. You mentioned your
      grandmother learning UPI last year. That could be your
      first line.
   Why: the opening is general and could be in any essay
```

(The student writes the new line. The skill points to their own material and does not write the essay for them.)

## Message going abroad

Message to a client in Germany: "Kindly do the needful and revert at the earliest."

Note:

```
This may confuse a reader outside India.
- "Do the needful" isn't used much elsewhere. Say what you need:
  "Please sign and return the form."
- "Revert" usually means "go back to an earlier state", not "reply".
  Try "Please reply by Friday."
Want me to rewrite it?
```
