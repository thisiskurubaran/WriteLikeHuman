# Event announcements

Announcements for weddings, engagements, births, and community or office events, written warmly and clearly.

## When this applies

- "Help me announce our engagement", "write a birth announcement", "post about our wedding date".
- Announcements for a society function, a shop opening, a school event, or an office celebration.
- A draft that sounds like a template ("We are thrilled and overjoyed to announce...").

For invitation cards and WhatsApp invites asking people to come, use `writing/invitations.md`. This module is for sharing news.

## What stays the same

- Every name, spelled exactly as the user gives it, including titles and family names.
- Dates, times, venues, and RSVP details, exactly. Never guess a weekday or a venue address.
- Religious and cultural terms the user uses (Nikah, Sangeet, Griha Pravesh, naming ceremony). Don't swap them for generic words.
- Birth details (weight, time, name) only if the user includes them.

## Steps

1. Work out the audience: close family, a big WhatsApp group, LinkedIn, a society notice board, or a printed card.
2. Lead with the news in one line. "Ananya and Rohit are engaged" says it better than a paragraph of build-up.
3. Add only the details the user gave: date, place, a line of thanks, how to RSVP or where to send wishes.
4. Keep the warmth in plain words. One real feeling is better than a stack of adjectives ("thrilled, overjoyed, and blessed").
5. Match the platform: short for WhatsApp, a little fuller for a card, no hashtag wall on social media.
6. If the date has a weekday, check it matches (see `adjust/before-send.md`).
7. Check every name, date, and time against what the user said.

## Things to avoid

- Inventing how the couple met, a quote from the parents, or a baby's weight.
- Forced positivity in announcements that mix news (a wedding after a recent loss). Keep it simple and let the user set the tone.
- Long blessings or religious lines the user didn't include.

## Output

The announcement, ready to post or print, then a short change summary. If something like the RSVP number is missing, leave a clear blank: `[RSVP number]`.

## Example

Before:

> We are absolutely thrilled and overjoyed to announce that with the blessings of our families, our beloved daughter Meera has embarked on a beautiful new journey and is now engaged to Arjun!!! The wedding will be on 14 February 2027 at Sri Krishna Mahal, Coimbatore.

After:

> With our families' blessings, Meera and Arjun are engaged. The wedding is on 14 February 2027 at Sri Krishna Mahal, Coimbatore. We'll share details soon.

What I changed
- Started with the news instead of the build-up.
- Cut "thrilled and overjoyed" and "embarked on a beautiful new journey".
- Kept both names, the date, and the venue exactly.
