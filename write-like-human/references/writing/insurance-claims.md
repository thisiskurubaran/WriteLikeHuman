# Insurance claims

Claim letters, follow-ups, and appeals to insurers, clear and exact.

## When this applies

- "Help me write a claim letter for my car accident", "my health claim was rejected, help me reply".
- Follow-ups on delayed claims, requests for a reason for rejection, and appeals.
- Claims for health, vehicle, home, travel, crop, or life insurance.

## What stays the same

- Policy number, claim number, dates, amounts, hospital or garage names, vehicle numbers, and names, exactly.
- What happened, as the user describes it. Never add details, injuries, damage, or costs.
- Never inflate a claim or suggest wording that could mislead the insurer. If the user asks for that, decline briefly and help with an honest version.
- The documents list: only documents the user actually has.

## Steps

1. Collect the facts: policy and claim numbers, what happened and when, the amount claimed, documents attached, and any reference from earlier calls or emails.
2. Open with the purpose and the numbers in the first lines, so the claims team can find the file: "Claim number HC-2026-48213, policy 1203/45/2025."
3. Describe what happened in date order, plainly.
4. List the attached documents.
5. State what you're asking for: processing, a reason for rejection in writing, a review, or payment by a date.
6. For a rejection, quote the insurer's stated reason and respond to it point by point using only the user's facts.
7. Remind the user to keep copies. In India, if the insurer doesn't respond or the user disagrees, they can escalate to the insurer's grievance officer, then to IRDAI's complaint system or the Insurance Ombudsman. Suggest checking the current details on the official sites.

## Output

The letter, ready to send, then the short reminders. Blanks for missing facts: `[claim number]`.

## Example

Before:

> Dear Sir, I want to bring to your kind notice that my claim is pending since long time and I have not got any update. Please do the needful at the earliest.

After:

> Subject: Delayed claim HC-2026-48213, policy 1203/45/2025
>
> Dear Claims Team,
>
> I submitted claim HC-2026-48213 on 2 September 2026 for my hospital stay at City Care Hospital from 21 to 24 August 2026. I have not received an update since.
>
> Please let me know the current status, and if any documents are missing, which ones.
>
> Regards,
> Anita Desai

What I changed
- Put the claim and policy numbers in the subject so the file is easy to find.
- Replaced "pending since long time" with the actual dates you gave.
- Swapped "do the needful" for a clear request.
