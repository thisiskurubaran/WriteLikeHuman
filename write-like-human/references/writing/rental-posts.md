# Rental and roommate posts

Help someone write a post for a flat to let, a PG or hostel room, or a roommate or flatmate wanted. These go on NoBroker, MagicBricks, 99acres, Facebook groups, OLX, office Slack channels, and WhatsApp groups.

## When this applies

- "Write a post to rent out my 2BHK."
- "Need a flatmate post for our office group."
- "Make my PG listing sound better."
- The user pastes a listing that is messy, all capitals, or full of filters.

## What stays the same

- Every fact the owner or tenant gives: rent, deposit, maintenance, area or locality, floor, size, furnishing, available-from date, lease length, parking, contact route.
- Real house rules that apply to everyone: no smoking indoors, no pets (if the building does not allow them), quiet hours, visitor timings in a PG, notice period.

Never invent features: "spacious", "sunlit", "near metro", "gated society", "24 hour water" only if the user said so. Never round down the rent or deposit, or leave out maintenance the user mentioned.

## Fair wording

Rental posts often include filters like "only vegetarians", "no bachelors", "only Hindus", "only South Indians", "no Muslims", "family only", or caste names. These shut people out for who they are, many platforms remove such posts, and some can be against the law.

- Do not add any such filter yourself.
- If the user's draft has one, rewrite the post without it and say so in one short, plain line: "I left out 'only vegetarians', since many sites remove posts that filter people this way. You can still mention house rules like no cooking non-veg in the shared kitchen, if that is the real concern."
- If the user insists, it is their post. Do not lecture. Keep your note to one line and do not repeat it.
- Behaviour-based rules are fine and useful: no smoking, no loud parties after 10 pm, two months' notice. Help phrase those clearly.

For roommate posts where people share a room, saying the gender of the person wanted for the shared room is common and practical. Keep it if the user includes it.

## Steps

1. Put the key facts first, in a short list: type (2BHK, PG single room, shared room), locality, rent, deposit, maintenance, available from, furnishing.
2. Add two to four lines of description using only what the user told you: what is nearby, what the flat or flatmates are like.
3. List house rules plainly.
4. End with how to contact and what to send ("message with your move-in date and where you work").
5. Fit the platform: short and line-broken for WhatsApp, a title plus details for listing sites.
6. Check every number and date against the original.

## Output

The post, ready to paste, with a title for listing sites. Then a short **What I changed** list, including one line about any filter you left out.

## Example

Before:

> 2BHK AVAILABLE KORAMANGALA!!! SPACIOUS SUNLIT FLAT!!! ONLY VEG FAMILIES. NO BACHELORS. RENT 32K DEPOSIT 2 LAKH. CONTACT ASAP

After:

> 2BHK for rent, Koramangala 6th Block
>
> - Rent: ₹32,000 a month
> - Deposit: ₹2,00,000
> - Semi-furnished, 2nd floor, available from 1 June
>
> Quiet building with a lift and covered parking for one car. Five minutes' walk to the bus stop on 80 Feet Road. No smoking inside the flat.
>
> Message me with your move-in date and how many people will stay.

What I changed
- Turned the capitals into a clear list with the rent and deposit exactly as you gave them.
- Left out "only veg families" and "no bachelors". Many sites remove posts that filter tenants this way. If cooking is the concern, I can add a kitchen rule instead.
- Dropped "spacious sunlit" and used the details you gave me about the building.

(The floor, parking, bus stop, and date were in the owner's notes in this example. Without them, leave blanks rather than adding them.)
