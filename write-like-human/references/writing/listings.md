# Product and listing descriptions

Help sellers describe what they sell in plain words. Meesho, Etsy, Amazon, Flipkart, OLX, Quikr, Facebook Marketplace, and Instagram shops.

## When this applies

- A seller pastes a listing that sounds like a template ("Upgrade your style with this premium product").
- They give notes about an item and want a title and description.
- Someone is selling a used item and wants an honest, clear listing.

## What stays the same

Every fact comes from the seller. Nothing else.

- Size, measurements, weight, colour, material, and quantity in the pack.
- Price, discounts, delivery charges, and return terms.
- Condition, age, and defects for second-hand items.
- Brand and model names, exactly as written.
- Care instructions and warranty terms.

## Never invent

- Materials or fabric percentages. If the seller says "cotton feel", do not write "100 percent cotton".
- Health, safety, or medical claims: "boosts immunity", "safe for babies", "chemical free", "dermatologist tested". Only include these if the seller says so and can stand behind it. Even then, keep the wording plain.
- Ratings, sales numbers, or "bestseller" labels.
- Uses the seller did not mention.

If something important is missing (size for clothes, dimensions for furniture), ask, or leave a clear blank like `[size]`.

## Second-hand items

Honest listings sell faster and get fewer returns. Keep every defect the seller mentions, in plain words, near the top. "Small scratch on the back panel, screen is perfect" is better than hiding it in the last line. Do not soften "does not turn on" into "needs minor attention".

## Steps

1. Pick out the facts: what it is, size, material, condition, price, what is included.
2. Write a title with the item, the key detail, and the size or model. Follow the platform's style: Amazon and Flipkart titles carry more detail, OLX and Instagram titles are short.
3. Start the description with what the buyer most needs to know. For clothes, that is fit and fabric. For used phones, it is condition and battery.
4. Use short lines or a small list for specs. Plain sentences for everything else.
5. Cut stock sales lines: "premium quality", "must-have", "perfect for every occasion", "upgrade your look".
6. Check every number and claim against the seller's notes.
7. Check platform limits in `lists/platform-limits.md` if the listing is for a social platform.

## Output

The title and the description, ready to paste, then a short change summary. List any blanks the seller still needs to fill.

## Example

Seller's draft (OLX):

> Premium quality study table in excellent condition!! Perfect for students and WFH. Sturdy and elegant design that will transform your workspace. Grab it before it's gone!!!

Seller's notes: "2 years old, engineered wood, 4 ft x 2 ft, one drawer, small chip on left corner, Rs 2,800, pickup only, Koramangala"

After:

> Title: Study table, 4 x 2 ft, engineered wood, one drawer
>
> Two years old and in good shape apart from a small chip on the left corner. Engineered wood, one drawer, fits a laptop and monitor side by side.
>
> Rs 2,800. Pickup only, from Koramangala.

What I changed
- Moved the size and material into the title.
- Mentioned the chip near the top so buyers are not surprised.
- Cut "premium", "elegant", "transform", and "grab it before it's gone".
- Kept the price, size, and area exactly as you gave them.

(The "laptop and monitor" line is fine only if the seller said so. Otherwise leave it out.)
