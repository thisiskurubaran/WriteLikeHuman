# Product FAQs

FAQ pages and answers for small businesses: shops, home bakeries, tutors, clinics, online sellers.

## When this applies

- "Write an FAQ for my website", "turn these customer questions into an FAQ page".
- An existing FAQ that sounds stiff or copied ("We are committed to providing the highest quality...").
- FAQ sections for a product listing, a Google Business profile, or a WhatsApp catalogue.

## What stays the same

- Every fact comes from the seller: prices, delivery areas, timings, return rules, ingredients, sizes, warranty terms. Never invent one.
- Policies stay exactly as strict or as loose as the seller wrote them. Don't make a "no returns" sound like "easy returns".
- Product names and brand names.
- Health, safety, or legal claims only if the seller gave them. Never add "doctor recommended" or "100 percent safe".

## Steps

1. Collect the real questions. If the seller has customer messages, use the questions people actually ask, in their words.
2. Ask for missing facts in one short list, or leave blanks: `[delivery charge]`.
3. Write each question the way a customer would type it: "Do you deliver to Whitefield?" not "What are your delivery coverage areas?"
4. Answer in one to three sentences. Start with yes, no, or the number.
5. Order by what people ask most, with price, delivery, and returns near the top.
6. Keep the seller's tone: warm for a home bakery, more formal for a clinic.

## Things to avoid

- Answers that repeat the question.
- Sales lines inside answers ("Our premium quality products...").
- Questions nobody asks, added to fill space.

## Output

The FAQ as question and answer pairs, ready to paste, then a short list of any blanks to fill.

## Example

Before:

> Q: What is your delivery policy?
> A: We are committed to ensuring timely and hassle-free delivery to our valued customers across various locations within the city limits.

After (seller's facts: deliver anywhere in Pune, Rs 50 charge, free above Rs 999, 2 to 3 days):

> Q: Do you deliver in Pune?
> A: Yes, anywhere in Pune, in 2 to 3 days. Delivery is Rs 50, and free on orders above Rs 999.
