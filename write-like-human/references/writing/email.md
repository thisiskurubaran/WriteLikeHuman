# Emails

## When this applies

The user pastes an email they wrote, an email they got and want to reply to, or asks you to write one. This covers work email, emails to a landlord or school, and personal email. For WhatsApp and SMS, use `writing/chat-replies.md` instead.

## What stays the same

- Every name, date, time, amount, order number, ticket number, and address.
- The ask. If the email asks for a refund by Friday, the rewrite still asks for a refund by Friday.
- The level of formality. An email to a bank manager stays formal. An email to a teammate stays relaxed.
- Anything the user quoted from an earlier email.
- Links and attachments mentioned in the text.

## Steps

1. Work out what the email is for. Most fit one of the presets below.
2. Put the point in the first two lines. People read email on phones and often stop early.
3. Cut warm-up lines that say nothing: "I hope this email finds you well", "I am writing to inform you that", "Please do not hesitate to reach out".
4. Replace stiff phrases with what a person would say. "Kindly revert at the earliest" can become "Could you reply by Thursday?" when the user's text gives that date. If it gives no date, leave the timing as vague as the user had it.
5. Keep paragraphs to two or three sentences. One idea each.
6. Check the subject line. It should say what the email is about in a few words.
7. Match the sign-off to the relationship (see below).
8. Re-check against `ai-patterns.md`, then write the change summary.

## Presets

Use these as a shape, not a script. Keep the user's own details.

**Quick reply.** One to three lines. Answer the question, confirm the next step, sign off. No greeting paragraph.

**Polite no.** Say no early and clearly. Give one honest reason if the user gave one, do not invent a reason. Offer an alternative only if the user mentioned one. Do not apologise more than once.

**Follow-up.** Mention the earlier email in one line ("Following up on my email from 3 March about the invoice"). Restate the ask. Give a date if the user has one. Do not guilt the reader.

**Thanks.** Say what the thanks is for, in specific words. "Thanks for staying late to fix the projector" beats "Thank you for your invaluable support".

**Apology.** Say what went wrong, own it in one sentence, say what happens next. Skip "We deeply regret any inconvenience this may have caused".

**Asking for something.** Say what you need, by when, and why it matters, in that order. Make it easy to say yes: one clear question, not five.

## Subject lines

- Say the topic: "Leave on 14 and 15 June", "Invoice 2231 still unpaid", "Can we move Tuesday's call?"
- No "Quick question" or "Following up" on its own. Add what it is about.
- No exclamation marks or all caps.
- Keep it under about 60 characters so it does not get cut off on a phone.

## Sign-offs

| Relationship | Good sign-offs |
|---|---|
| Teammate, friend | Thanks, / Cheers, / Just the name |
| Client, manager, someone new | Thanks, / Best, / Regards, |
| Bank, government office, formal complaint | Regards, / Yours sincerely, |

Indian work email often uses "Regards" or "Thanks and regards". That is normal, keep it if the user wrote it. Do not swap it for an American style sign-off.

Avoid "Best wishes and warm regards", "Looking forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience", and stacked closings.

## Output

The rewritten email with its subject line, ready to copy. Then the change summary. If you are replying to an email the user received, give only the reply, not the original.

## Example

Before:

```
Subject: Following up

Dear Mr. Rao,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to follow up on my previous
email regarding the pending payment. I wanted to kindly remind you that
invoice number 4417 for Rs. 38,500 is still pending. Please do not hesitate
to reach out if you have any questions or concerns. I look forward to your
prompt response.

Warm regards,
Neha
```

After:

```
Subject: Invoice 4417 still pending

Dear Mr. Rao,

Following up on my earlier email: invoice 4417 for Rs. 38,500 is still
unpaid. Could you let me know when it will be cleared?

Happy to resend the invoice if that helps.

Regards,
Neha
```

What I changed
- Put the invoice number in the subject so it is easy to find.
- Cut the opening and closing lines that did not add anything.
- Turned the reminder into a direct question.
- Kept the invoice number, amount, and names exactly as you had them.
