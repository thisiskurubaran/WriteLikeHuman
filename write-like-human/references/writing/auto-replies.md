# Auto-replies and signatures

Help people write the short messages that go out on their own: out-of-office email replies, holiday notices, WhatsApp Business away and greeting messages, and email signatures.

## When this applies

- "Write my out-of-office reply, I'm on leave from 12 to 20 May."
- A shop wants a WhatsApp Business away message for Sundays or a festival closure.
- Someone wants a greeting message for new customers on WhatsApp Business.
- Someone wants a cleaner email signature.
- They paste an auto-reply that sounds stiff ("I am currently out of the office with limited access to email").

## What stays the same

- Dates, return day, opening hours, and time zones, exactly as given.
- Backup contacts: names, email addresses, and phone numbers, character for character.
- Job titles, company names, and addresses in signatures.
- The user's choice of what to share. If they don't give a reason for being away, don't add one.

## Privacy

- Don't add personal reasons ("I'm in hospital", "attending a funeral") unless the user asks. "I'm away" is enough.
- Auto-replies go to everyone who writes, including strangers. Don't include a personal phone number unless the user wants it there.

## Steps

1. Collect the facts: away from and until, who to contact instead, what's urgent and what can wait, opening hours for a shop.
2. Put the key facts in the first line: "I'm away until Monday 20 May."
3. Give one clear next step: "For anything urgent, write to Ravi at ravi@northbay.in."
4. Keep it short. Two to four lines for email, one or two for WhatsApp.
5. Signatures: name, role, company, one phone or email, and a link if they want one. No quotes, no long legal text the user didn't ask for, no images as text.
6. Check the weekday matches the date if both are given. If they don't match, ask.
7. For festival closures, keep the greeting simple and say when the shop reopens.

## Output

The message or signature, ready to paste. For WhatsApp Business, mention that away and greeting messages are set in the app's business tools, without step-by-step menus that may change.

## Example

Before:

> Thank you for your email. I am currently out of the office with limited access to email and will respond to your message upon my return. For urgent matters, please contact my colleague.

After:

> Thanks for your email. I'm on leave until Monday 20 May and won't be checking mail. For anything urgent, please write to Ravi Kumar at ravi@northbay.in.

What I changed
- Put the return date first, since that's what people need.
- Named the colleague and their email, which the original left out.
- Cut "limited access" and "upon my return", which said the same thing twice.
