# School circulars

Circulars and notices from a school to parents, written so busy parents understand them in one read.

## When this applies

- Teachers, principals, or school office staff writing circulars about holidays, fees, exams, trips, events, uniforms, or timings.
- A circular that sounds stiff or copied ("It is hereby informed to all the parents that...").
- Notices for the school WhatsApp group, the diary, or the website.

For messages from a parent to a teacher, use `writing/school-messages.md`.

## What stays the same

- Dates, days, timings, fees, amounts, deadlines, class names, and sections, exactly.
- Rules and requirements as the school states them. Don't soften a "compulsory" into "encouraged".
- Names of people, the school, and events.
- The school's circular number and reference format, if it uses one.

## Steps

1. Put the main point in the first line: what is happening, for whom, and when.
2. List the details parents need to act on: date, time, what to bring or pay, the deadline, and who to contact. A short list works well here.
3. Use plain, kind words. Many parents read English as a second language, so keep sentences short (see `adjust/reading-level.md` if asked).
4. If the date has a weekday, check it matches (`adjust/before-send.md`).
5. Keep the closing short: who it's from, and a contact for questions.
6. For WhatsApp, give a shorter version that fits on one phone screen.

## Things to avoid

- Openers like "It is hereby informed" or "This is to bring to your kind notice".
- Burying the date or the fee in the third paragraph.
- Threats or harsh language about late fees. State the rule plainly.

## Output

The circular, ready to print or send, and a short WhatsApp version if it's going to a group. Then a brief change summary.

## Example

Before:

> It is hereby informed to all the parents of Class 5 that the school will be organising an educational excursion to the Science Centre on 22/11/2026 (Sunday) for which a contribution of Rs 350 is to be paid on or before 18/11/2026 positively.

After:

> Class 5 trip to the Science Centre
>
> Date: 22 November 2026
> Cost: Rs 350, to be paid by 18 November 2026
>
> Please send the amount with your child, or pay at the school office.

Check before sending: 22 November 2026 is a Sunday. Is that the right date for a school trip?

(The "Check before sending" line is shown because the weekday matched but a Sunday trip is unusual. In real use, only flag weekdays that don't match the date or look odd.)
