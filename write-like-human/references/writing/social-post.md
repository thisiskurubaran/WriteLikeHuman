# Social media posts

## When this applies

LinkedIn posts, Instagram captions, X posts and threads, Facebook posts, WhatsApp status updates, and similar. Always load `lists/platform-limits.md` alongside this file. For profile bios, use `writing/bios.md` instead.

## What stays the same

- Facts, names, tags of real people and companies, dates, numbers, links.
- The user's news. A job change stays a job change, a launch stays a launch.
- Hashtags the user clearly needs (an event hashtag, a company campaign tag).
- Emoji the user chose, unless there are so many they get in the way. Do not add new ones.
- The platform. Do not turn a LinkedIn post into an Instagram caption unless asked.

## AI tells on social media

These show up constantly and make posts read as generated:

- One sentence per line, every line. The LinkedIn style where each sentence gets its own paragraph and the post scrolls forever. Group related sentences into short paragraphs.
- The hook formula. "I almost didn't post this." "Here's what nobody tells you about..." "This changed everything." Start with the actual news or thought.
- Lesson lists. "3 things I learned from getting fired:" followed by tidy numbered lessons. Fine if the user really has three points. If it was forced into a list, write it as a paragraph.
- Engagement bait. "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Comment YES if...", "Repost if this resonated". Cut unless the user really wants replies, then ask a real question.
- Hashtag walls. Ten or more tags at the bottom. Keep the few that matter (see the platform file for norms).
- Humble-brag framing. "I'm humbled and honoured to announce" for a new job. "Happy to share that I've joined..." is how people actually say it.
- Grand closers. "The future belongs to those who dare." Cut it, or end on something specific.

## Platform notes

**LinkedIn.** Professional but human. Short paragraphs, not single lines. Lead with the news in the first line or two, since the post is cut off after about two lines with "see more". Three to five hashtags at most.

**Instagram.** The caption supports the photo. Short is usually better. Hashtags can go at the end or in the first comment. Line breaks are fine.

**X.** One clear thought per post. For threads, each post should make sense alone. No "A thread 🧵" opener unless the user wants it.

**Facebook.** Conversational, written for friends and family. Longer is fine for personal news.

**WhatsApp status.** Very short. A line or two, often with a photo.

## Steps

1. Identify the platform and the point of the post.
2. Move the actual news or idea to the top.
3. Merge single-line paragraphs into short paragraphs where they belong together.
4. Cut hooks, bait, and closers that add nothing.
5. Trim hashtags to what the platform norm and the user need.
6. Check the length against `lists/platform-limits.md`.
7. Re-check against `ai-patterns.md`, then write the change summary.

## Output

The post, ready to paste. Keep line breaks as they should appear on the platform. Then the change summary.

## Example

Before (LinkedIn):

```
I have some news. 🚀

After 4 incredible years at Northwind Foods...

I'm humbled and thrilled to announce...

That I've accepted a new role as Operations Lead at Greenfield Logistics!

Here are 3 things I learned along the way:
1. Growth happens outside your comfort zone
2. People are everything
3. Never stop learning

Agree? 👇

#newjob #career #growth #leadership #operations #logistics #success #grateful #motivation #journey
```

After:

```
Some news: I've joined Greenfield Logistics as Operations Lead. 🚀

I spent the last 4 years at Northwind Foods, and I'm grateful to the team
there, especially the warehouse crew in Nashik who taught me most of what I
know about running a site.

Looking forward to starting this new chapter.

#newjob #operations #logistics
```

What I changed
- Put the news in the first line so it shows before "see more".
- Joined the one-line paragraphs into normal short paragraphs.
- Dropped the three generic lessons and the "Agree?" line.
- Cut the hashtags from ten to three.
- Kept the rocket emoji you added, both company names, and the 4 years.

The Nashik detail is in the "After" only because the user mentioned it. Never add a detail like that on your own.
