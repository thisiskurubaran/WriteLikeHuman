# Marathi

Rewrite Marathi so it sounds like a person wrote it, in the register the situation needs.

## When this applies

- The text is in Marathi, in Devanagari or in Roman script.
- The user asks for a translation into Marathi (also load `translate-humanize.md`).
- For Marathi mixed with English, load `mixed-language.md` as well.

## What stays the same

- Language and script. Marathi stays Marathi. Never translate unless asked. Do not drift into Hindi words or grammar.
- Names, numbers, dates, amounts, and places.
- The register. "तुम्ही" or "आपण" stays respectful. "तू" stays informal.
- Everyday English words people use in Marathi (phone, bus, office), unless the user avoids them.

## AI tells in Marathi

- Hindi words slipping in. "इंतज़ार" or "लेकिन" where Marathi uses "वाट पाहणे" or "पण". This is a common machine error.
- Textbook Sanskrit-based words in casual text.
- Literal English calques, such as word for word "I hope this finds you well".
- Wrong level of respect for the reader.
- Repeated sentence shape.

Official letters stay formal. Fix stiffness, keep the register.

## Steps

1. Work out the reader and keep the user's register.
2. Remove Hindi words and grammar that do not belong.
3. Rewrite calques the way a Marathi speaker would say them.
4. Vary sentence length.
5. Check names, numbers, and dates are unchanged.

## Output

The rewritten Marathi text, then a short **What I changed** list in the language the user is using with you.

## Example

Before (to a friend): "मित्रा, मी उद्या येणार आहे याची मी तुला सूचना देतो."

After: "उद्या येतोय रे."

Gloss: The first reads like an official notice: "Friend, I give you notice that I am going to come tomorrow." The second sounds like a real text: "Coming tomorrow."
