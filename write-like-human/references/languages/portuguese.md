# Portuguese

Rewrite Portuguese so it sounds like a person wrote it, for the right country and reader.

## When this applies

- The text is in Portuguese.
- The user asks for a translation into Portuguese (also load `translate-humanize.md`). Ask Brazil or Portugal if it is not clear.

## What stays the same

- Language and variety. Brazilian Portuguese stays Brazilian, European Portuguese stays European. Never translate unless asked.
- Forms of address. In Brazil "você" is normal and "tu" is regional. In Portugal "tu" is informal and "você" can sound cold; formal writing often uses "o senhor", "a senhora", or the verb alone. Keep what the user uses.
- Local words: "celular" or "telemóvel", "tela" or "ecrã", "trem" or "comboio", "ônibus" or "autocarro".
- Names, numbers, dates, amounts, and places. Keep the number format (1.500,50) and date format (dd/mm/aaaa).

## AI tells in Portuguese

- Mixing Brazil and Portugal in one text: "você" with "telemóvel", or Brazilian spelling with European grammar.
- English calques: "aplicar para uma vaga" (use "candidatar-se"), "endereçar um problema" (use "resolver" or "tratar"), "realizar" for "perceber" in the sense of noticing.
- Translated openers: "Espero que este e-mail o encontre bem", "Venho por meio deste informar".
- "Gerundismo" in Brazil: "vou estar enviando" instead of "vou enviar".
- Capitalized days and months copied from English. In Portuguese they are lowercase: "segunda-feira", "março".
- Overuse of the passive with "ser": "foi aprovado pela equipe" instead of "a equipe aprovou".

## Steps

1. Identify the country and register, and keep both.
2. Remove calques, translated openers, and gerundismo.
3. Make the vocabulary consistent with one country.
4. Fix capitalization and accents.
5. Check names, numbers, and dates are unchanged.

## Output

The rewritten Portuguese text, then a short **What I changed** list in the language the user is using with you.

## Example

Before: "Prezado Carlos, espero que este e-mail o encontre bem. Venho por meio deste informar que a reunião foi movida para Segunda-feira."

After: "Oi Carlos, tudo bem? Passamos a reunião para segunda-feira."

Gloss: Brazilian Portuguese. "Hi Carlos, how are you? We moved the meeting to Monday." The first uses a translated opener, a stiff formula, a passive, and a capital on "Segunda-feira". Keep the formal tone if the user writes to Carlos formally.
