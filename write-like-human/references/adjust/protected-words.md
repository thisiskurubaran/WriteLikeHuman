# Protected words

Keep a list of words the user never wants changed, and leave every one of them exactly as written.

## When this applies

- The user gives a list: "Don't change these words", "keep 'FinEdge Pro' as is", "always spell my name Shreeya".
- Their voice profile has a "Never change these words" section.
- They complain that an earlier rewrite changed a term ("you changed our product name again").

Typical entries: brand and product names, product codes, team or project names, technical terms and jargon the readers expect, a child's or pet's nickname, a place name spelled the local way, and the user's own spelling of their name.

## What stays the same

- Every protected term, character for character: spelling, capitals, spacing, hyphens, and punctuation inside the term. "eKart" stays "eKart", not "E-Kart" or "Ekart".
- Everything the core rules already protect: names, numbers, dates, amounts, quotes.
- The rest of the rewrite works as usual. A protected term is not a reason to skip fixing the sentence around it.

## Steps

1. Collect the list. Take it from the message, the voice profile, or both. If the user says "keep our usual terms" but never gave a list, ask for it in one line.
2. Note how each term is written, exactly. If a term appears in the text in two different spellings, ask which one is right instead of picking.
3. Do the rewrite or draft as usual. Treat each protected term as fixed text you build sentences around.
4. Leave protected terms alone in every place they appear: body text, headings, tables, spreadsheet cells, slide notes, file names, and hashtags.
5. Before returning, check every term on the list against the result. Each one must appear the same number of times as in the original, spelled the same way, unless you removed a whole sentence the user agreed to cut.
6. If a protected term itself reads oddly (jargon in a message to a parent), keep it and mention it in the change summary instead of changing it: "Kept 'KYC' as you asked. Your mom may not know it; want me to add a short explanation in brackets?"

## Keeping the list for next time

You cannot remember the list between conversations. Suggest the user keep it in their voice profile under "Never change these words" (see `voice/voice-profile-template.md`), or in a note they paste in. One line is enough: "Save this list with your voice profile and I'll follow it every time."

## Output

The rewritten text or file as usual. In the change summary, add one line only if it matters: "Kept all 6 of your protected terms exactly as written." Do not list every term back unless asked.

## Example

Protected words: "Kuttu" (son's nickname), "PayNest", "SIP".

Before:

> I'm writing to inform you that Kuttu will be absent tomorrow as he has a medical appointment. Furthermore, I wanted to confirm that the fees have been paid via PayNest, ensuring timely processing.

After:

> Kuttu won't be in school tomorrow because he has a doctor's appointment. I've also paid the fees through PayNest.

What I changed
- Started with the actual news instead of "I'm writing to inform you".
- Cut "Furthermore" and "ensuring timely processing".
- Kept "Kuttu" and "PayNest" exactly as you write them.
