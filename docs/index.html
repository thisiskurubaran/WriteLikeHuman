Hi team, the quarterly report deadline has moved to 15 March. Please send me your inputs by then.
What I changed

Install in two minutes

  1. Download WriteLikeHuman.zip from the latest release. Don't unzip it.
  2. In Claude, open Settings > Capabilities and turn on Code execution and file creation.
  3. Go to Customize > Skills, click +, then Upload a skill, and pick the zip.
  4. Check the switch next to WriteLikeHuman is on. That's it.

Once it's on your account, it works in the Claude phone app too. Full steps are in the install guide.

What it handles

Your files

Word, PDF, Google Docs, spreadsheets, slides, text, subtitles, web pages. Formatting, numbers, and formulas stay put.

Everyday writing

Emails, WhatsApp replies, letters, resumes, posts, speeches, wishes, reviews, invitations, complaints, and more.

Your own voice

Learns how you write from your own samples, so the result sounds like you, not like a template.

Many languages

Indian English, Hinglish, Tanglish, and 27 other languages from India, the rest of Asia, and Europe. It never translates unless you ask.

Checks before you send

Spots a missing attachment, a weekday that doesn't match the date, or a question you forgot to answer.

Honest by design

Keeps your facts. No fake reviews, no invented experiences, no pretending to be someone else.
