What it fixes

The habits that give chatbot writing away

One habit on its own proves nothing. A stack of them is what makes a message sound generated. WriteLikeHuman spots the stack and fixes it without making your writing sloppy.

Throat-clearing openers

In today's fast-paced world, staying organized matters.

Here's how I keep track of bills.

Filler phrases

It's important to note that the office is closed.

The office is closed on Monday.

Inflated importance

A significant milestone in our journey.

The canteen has a new menu.

Chatbot sign-offs

I hope this helps! Feel free to reach out!

Thanks, Arjun

Forced groups of three

Dedicated, passionate, and committed to excellence.

Our team cares about getting it right.

Fancy words for plain ones

We will commence once all participants arrive.

We'll start once everyone is here.

Features

One skill, everything you write

You upload one zip once. Claude picks the right approach from what you give it and what you ask for.

Your files, kept intact

Word, PDF, Google Docs, spreadsheets, slides, and web pages. Fonts, tables, and formulas stay put, and you get a new copy.

29 kinds of writing

Emails, WhatsApp replies, Indian format letters, resumes, posts, speeches, wishes, complaints, RTI applications, and more.

Sounds like you

Share a few things you wrote and it builds a voice profile. Keep a "work me" and a "personal me".

Check before you send

Spots a missing attachment, a weekday that doesn't match the date, or a question you forgot to answer.

Tone, length, reading level

Friendlier, more formal, simpler for your dad, or cut to 150 words. It keeps the meaning and the facts.

Private and honest

Your text stays in your chat. It never invents facts, and it won't write fake reviews.

Languages

Install

Ready in two minutes

If you can upload a photo, you can install this. Skills you add on the website show up in the Claude phone app too.

Download the zip

Get WriteLikeHuman.zip from the latest release. Don't unzip it.

Turn on file creation

In Claude, open Settings > Capabilities and turn on Code execution and file creation.

Upload the skill

Go to Customize > Skills, click +, choose Upload a skill, and pick the zip.

Using Claude Code? Install it as a plugin 
/plugin marketplace add thisiskurubaran/WriteLikeHuman
/plugin install write-like-human@writelikehuman

Stuck? The install guide has every step, also in हिंदी and தமிழ்.

Honest by design

Better writing, not pretending

WriteLikeHuman helps you say what you mean in your own words. Some things it simply won't do.

  • Change your names, numbers, dates, amounts, or quotes
  • Promise that writing will get past any checker
  • Write fake reviews, or reviews of things you haven't used
  • Add experiences or qualifications you don't have
  • Imitate a real person other than you

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. WriteLikeHuman is free and open source under the MIT license. You need a Claude account where skills are available.

Will it mess up my document?

It changes words, not layout. Fonts, tables, lists, and formulas stay put, and you always get a new copy named with "-humanized", so your original is untouched.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Upload it once on the Claude website or desktop app, then use it in the Claude phone app with the same account.

It didn't kick in. What now?

Start your message with "Use WriteLikeHuman to...". If that doesn't help, check the switch is on under Customize > Skills.

Does it keep my writing private?

Your text is only used in your own chat with Claude. The skill doesn't save or send it anywhere.
